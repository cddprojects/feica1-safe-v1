How to search for remote work

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

Pick a job title that matches your skills, then let Job Bank’s remote and hours filters narrow the list.

Job Bank search options

  • A job title that fits your skills, not just the words “work from home.”
  • Job Bank’s remote filter, if you want a fully remote job.
  • An hours filter for work alongside a main job.
  • A job alert, so new matches land in your inbox.
  • The employer’s own careers page, before you apply.

What Job Bank means by remote

Job Bank lets employers label a job in one of four ways. Remote means the work is done from a place you choose, like a home office. Hybrid means part in person and part remote, with the split agreed with the employer. On-site means a place the employer names, with no remote option. On the road means the location changes because the job involves travel.

Searching only for “work from home” can bring up hybrid and on-site postings that mention a home elsewhere in the text. For a fully remote job, use the filter instead. On Job Bank’s Find a job page, enter a job title and location, then choose Remote under work location.

Hours for a second job

The same results page can filter by hours. For someone with a main job, the part-time filter narrows results by weekly hours; part-time work may be a main or additional job. Statistics Canada counts part-time as under 30 hours a week at the main job, and a second job can involve far fewer. Weekly hours or project deadlines are normally included in the posting. Full-time remote work is a different search, since it is usually a main job done from home.

Remote results may come from across the country because the work is not tied to the city entered in the search. The province in each posting still matters, but it may not by itself determine which employment standards apply. Check the relevant provincial, territorial or federal authority. See pay and deductions for additional work.

Job alerts

When available, save the filtered search or create an alert for new matches.

Before applying, verify that the position also appears on the employer’s website and compare the employer, location, hours and pay details. The job-posting guide provides a checklist.
