Where to look

Where to look for this work in Canada

North Desk · Last reviewed October 2026

Job Bank is the public board. The remote filter and the hours filter are how you ask it for a second path, rather than for every job in the country.

Search in this order

  • A job title you can already do, not the words “work from home” alone.
  • Job Bank’s remote filter, if the seat must not be an office.
  • An hours filter, if this work sits beside a main job.
  • A job alert, so the next match arrives by email.
  • The employer’s own careers page, before you apply.

What Job Bank means by remote

Job Bank lets an employer mark a job in one of four ways. Remote means the work must be done remotely, from a place you choose, such as a home office. Hybrid means some of the work is in person and some is remote, and the split is discussed with the employer. On-site means a place the employer names, with no remote option. On the road means the location keeps changing because of travel.

A search for the words “work from home” is looser. It can return hybrid and on-site ads that mention a home somewhere in the text. If you need the seat to be remote, use the filter. Open Find a job, enter a title and a location, then choose Remote under work location.

Hours, for a second job

The same results page can filter by hours of work. If you already have a main job, part-time is the filter that matches a second block of hours. In the Labour Force Survey, part-time means usually under 30 hours a week at the main job. Your second job can be well under that. A posting should still say the weekly hours or the deadline. Full-time remote is a different search: it is a main job you would do from home, not an add-on.

Job Bank also says remote results can appear from across the country, because the work is not tied to the city you typed. Read the province on the card. Job Bank’s own note is that remote jobs follow the wage rules of the province where the employer is located, and that those rules can differ from the province where you live. Pay and province is the page for that check.

Save the search

After the filters look right, Job Bank can email you when a new posting matches. On the results page, choose the alert for that search and confirm the email address they send you. You can change or stop the alert later. The Government of Canada’s board is free to use.

Then open the employer named on the card and find the same role on that employer’s site when you can. Apply there or on Job Bank. North Desk does not carry the postings and does not introduce you. When you have an ad open, read it with the field order.
