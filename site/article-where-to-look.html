How to search for remote work

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

Pick a job title that matches your skills, then let Job Bank’s remote and hours filters narrow the list.

A simple search routine

  • A job title that fits your skills, not just the words “work from home.”
  • Job Bank’s remote filter, if you want a fully remote job.
  • An hours filter, if it’s going beside a main job.
  • A job alert, so new matches land in your inbox.
  • The employer’s own careers page, before you apply.

What Job Bank means by remote

Job Bank lets employers label a job in one of four ways. Remote means the work is done from a place you choose, like a home office. Hybrid means part in person and part remote, with the split agreed with the employer. On-site means a place the employer names, with no remote option. On the road means the location changes because the job involves travel.

Searching the words “work from home” is hit and miss, because it can bring up hybrid and on-site postings that mention a home somewhere in the text. For a fully remote job, use the filter instead. On Job Bank’s Find a job page, enter a job title and location, then choose Remote under work location.

Hours, if it’s a second job

The same results page can filter by hours. If you already have a main job, the part-time filter is a good way to find extra hours. Statistics Canada counts part-time as under 30 hours a week at the main job, and a second job can be far fewer. The posting should still tell you the weekly hours or deadlines. Full-time remote work is a different search, since it is usually a main job done from home.

Remote results can come from across the country, because the work isn’t tied to the city you typed in. Check the province in each posting. Job Bank notes that remote jobs follow the wage rules of the province where the employer is located, which may not be where you live. More on that in What a second paycheque changes.

Set up a job alert

Once your filters look right, Job Bank can email you when a new posting appears. Create a job alert from the results page, then confirm it using the email Job Bank sends you. You can change or stop it at any time, and Job Bank is free.

When a posting looks promising, find the employer’s name and see if the same job is on its website. You can apply there or through Job Bank. We don’t post jobs or introduce candidates to employers. Got one open? Here is how to read it.
