How to search for remote work

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

Job Bank searches typically begin with a job title matched to skills; remote and hours filters narrow results more reliably than generic phrases.

Job Bank search options

  • A job title that fits your skills, not just the words “work from home.”
  • Job Bank’s remote filter, if you want a fully remote job.
  • An hours filter for work alongside a main job.
  • A job alert, so new matches land in your inbox.
  • The employer’s own careers page, before you apply.

What Job Bank means by remote

Job Bank lets employers label a job in one of four ways. Remote means the work is done from a place you choose, like a home office. Hybrid means part in person and part remote, with the split agreed with the employer. On-site means a place the employer names, with no remote option. On the road means the location changes because the job involves travel.

Searching only for “work from home” can bring up hybrid and on-site postings that mention a home elsewhere in the text. For fully remote work, the work-location filter is more reliable than keywords alone. On Job Bank’s Find a job page, the search uses a job title and location, with Remote selected under work location.

Hours for a second job

The same results page can filter by hours. For someone with a main job, the part-time filter narrows the search to additional hours. Statistics Canada counts part-time as under 30 hours a week at the main job, and a second job can involve far fewer. Weekly hours or project deadlines are normally included in the posting. Full-time remote work is a different search, since it is usually a main job done from home.

Remote results may list employers across Canada regardless of the city entered in the search. Province and employer location still matter for eligibility and pay rules; details are covered in What a second paycheque changes.

Job alerts

Job Bank can send email when a new posting matches saved filters. An alert is created from the results page and confirmed through the email Job Bank sends. Alerts can be changed or stopped, and Job Bank is free.

The same vacancy is often listed on the employer’s careers site; applications may go through that site or Job Bank. Posting details are covered in the job-posting guide.
