How to search for remote work

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

Start with an occupation or role for which you are qualified, then use Job Bank’s remote and hours filters to narrow the results.

Search in this order

  • An occupation or role for which you are qualified, not the words “work from home” alone.
  • Job Bank’s remote filter, if you need a fully remote position.
  • An hours filter, if this work sits beside a main job.
  • A job alert, so the next match arrives by email.
  • The employer’s own careers page, before you apply.

What Job Bank means by remote

Job Bank lets an employer mark a job in one of four ways. Remote means the work must be done remotely, from a place you choose, such as a home office. Hybrid means some of the work is in person and some is remote, and the split is discussed with the employer. On-site means a place the employer names, with no remote option. On the road means the location keeps changing because of travel.

A search for the words “work from home” is less precise. It can return hybrid and on-site postings that mention a home somewhere in the text. If you need a fully remote position, use the filter. Open Find a job, enter a title and a location, then choose Remote under work location.

Hours, for a second job

The same results page can filter by hours of work. If you already have a main job, the part-time filter can help you find additional working hours. In the Labour Force Survey, part-time generally means fewer than 30 hours a week at the main job. A second job can involve far fewer hours. A posting should still state the weekly hours or deadlines. Full-time remote work is a different search: it is generally a main job done from home, not additional work.

Job Bank also says remote results can appear from across the country because the work is not tied to the city you entered. Read the province in the posting. Job Bank notes that remote jobs follow the wage rules of the province where the employer is located, which may differ from the rules where you live. Read Pay and province for more context.

Save the search

Once the filters match your needs, Job Bank can email you when a new posting appears. On the results page, create a job alert and confirm it through the email Job Bank sends you. You can change or stop the alert later. Job Bank is free to use.

Note the employer named in the posting and, when possible, find the same position on the employer’s website. Apply there or through Job Bank. Job Compass does not publish job postings or introduce candidates to employers. When you have a posting open, learn how to review it with confidence.
