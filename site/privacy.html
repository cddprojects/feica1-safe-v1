Privacy

Privacy

Last updated 27 October 2026.

North Desk is an independent publisher. We do not run accounts, and we do not ask you to create one. This notice describes the little personal information the site can involve. It is written with the fair-information principles in Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA) in mind. Provincial privacy laws, including Quebec’s, can also apply depending on where you are and how a message is handled.

What you can send us

The contact form does not submit to our server. It opens your own email application with a message addressed to desk@northdesk.ca. If you send that email, we receive whatever you chose to include: typically your name, email address, province and message. We use it to reply, to correct an article, or to consider a topic. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to build a marketing list.

Do not send a social insurance number, banking details, identity documents or passwords. We will not ask for them.

What stays on your device

The scam check and the glossary search run in your browser. What you type into them is not transmitted to us by those tools.

Fonts and server logs

Pages request typefaces from Google Fonts. That request can expose your IP address to Google. A host that serves these files may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address, browser and page requested, for security. We do not add our own analytics tracker to these pages.

How long a message is kept

If you email us, we keep the thread long enough to answer and to keep a correction on file, then delete it or reduce it to an anonymous note. You can ask what we still have, ask for a correction, or ask us to delete the thread, by writing to desk@northdesk.ca. We may need to confirm we are writing back to the same address.

Other sites

Articles link to Job Bank, Statistics Canada, provincial pages and the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre. Those sites have their own notices. We do not control them.
