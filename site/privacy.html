Privacy

Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

This notice explains the limited personal information involved when you use Job Compass. The site does not offer user accounts.

What you can send us

The contact form creates an email in your email application; it does not submit information to this website. If you send the message, Job Compass receives the information included in it, such as your name, email address, province or territory, topic and message. That information is used to respond and, where relevant, improve published guides. Contact information is not sold or added to a marketing list.

Do not send a social insurance number, banking details, identification or passwords. Job Compass will not ask for them.

What stays on your device

The path check and the glossary search run in your browser. What you choose or type there is not transmitted to us by those tools.

Fonts and server logs

Pages request typefaces from Google Fonts. That request can expose your IP address to Google. Our hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address, browser and page requested, for security. We do not add our own analytics tracker to these pages.

How long a message is kept

We keep emails only as long as we need them to reply, keep a record or meet a legal obligation. After that, we delete or anonymize them. You can ask what information we still have, request a correction or ask us to delete the thread by writing to hello@jobcompass.ca. We may need to verify that the request comes from the email account concerned.

Other sites

Articles link to Job Bank, Statistics Canada, the Canada Revenue Agency and provincial pages. Those sites have their own notices. We do not control them.
