Personal information

Privacy

This policy explains how personal information is handled when the site is used or an inquiry is submitted. Last updated 29 October 2026.

Job Compass is an independent publisher. No account is required to read the site. Personal information is limited to what a visitor chooses to provide and the technical information needed to operate and protect the site.

Information collected

An inquiry may include a name, email address, province or territory, selected topic and message. Social insurance numbers, banking details, identification, passwords and other sensitive information should not be submitted. Job Compass does not request this information.

The hosting provider may process technical information such as an IP address, browser type, date and time, and the page requested. This information may be used to deliver the site, maintain security and diagnose technical problems.

How information is used

Inquiry information is used to respond, maintain correspondence, review source corrections and improve the published guides. It is not sold, rented or added to a marketing list.

Local features and external services

The glossary search runs in the browser, and search terms are not submitted to Job Compass. Pages request typefaces from Google Fonts, which may receive technical information when a font is loaded. Google and the hosting provider handle information under their own privacy terms.

Retention and safeguards

Personal information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary to respond, maintain an appropriate record or meet a legal requirement. It is then deleted or de-identified where appropriate.

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are used to protect personal information. No method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Access and correction

Requests to access or correct personal information, or questions about this policy, may be sent to hello@jobcompass.ca. Identity may need to be confirmed before a request is completed. Requests are handled subject to applicable Canadian privacy law.

Links to other sites

Articles link to government, labour-market and provincial or territorial websites. Job Compass does not control their privacy practices, and each site has its own privacy terms.
