Privacy

How information is handled when the site or contact form is used. Last updated 29 October 2026.

Job Compass is an independent publisher. The site has no user accounts. This notice describes the limited personal information that may be involved when the site is used. It reflects the fair information principles in Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Provincial privacy laws, including Quebec’s, may also apply depending on the reader’s location and how a message is handled.

Contact inquiries

An inquiry may include a name, email address, province and question. This information is used to respond to the inquiry and improve the guides. It is not sold or added to a marketing list.

Social insurance numbers, banking details, identification and passwords should not be included. Job Compass does not request them.

Information that stays on the device

The glossary search runs in the browser. Search terms are not transmitted to Job Compass by that tool.

Fonts and server logs

Pages request typefaces from Google Fonts. That request can expose the reader’s IP address to Google. The hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address, browser and page requested, for security. Job Compass does not add an analytics tracker to these pages.

How long a message is kept

Email is kept only as long as needed to reply, maintain a record or meet a legal obligation. It is then deleted or anonymised. A request to access, correct or delete a message can be sent to hello@jobcompass.ca. Verification may be required from the email account concerned.

Other sites

Articles link to government, labour-market and provincial websites. Those sites have separate privacy notices and are not controlled by Job Compass.
