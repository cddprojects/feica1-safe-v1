Privacy

Privacy

Last updated 28 October 2026.

North Desk is an independent publisher. We do not offer user accounts or ask you to create one. This notice describes the limited personal information that may be involved when you use the site. It reflects the fair information principles in Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). Provincial privacy laws, including Quebec’s, may also apply depending on where you are and how a message is handled.

What you can send us

The contact form does not submit to our server. It opens your own email application with a message addressed to desk@northdesk.ca. If you send that email, we receive whatever you chose to include: typically your name, email address, province and message. We use it to reply, to correct an article, or to consider a topic. We do not sell it, and we do not use it to build a marketing list.

Do not send a social insurance number, banking details, identity documents or passwords. We will not ask for them.

What stays on your device

The path check and the glossary search run in your browser. What you choose or type there is not transmitted to us by those tools.

Fonts and server logs

Pages request typefaces from Google Fonts. That request can expose your IP address to Google. Our hosting provider may keep ordinary server logs, such as IP address, browser and page requested, for security. We do not add our own analytics tracker to these pages.

How long a message is kept

We retain editorial email only while it is needed to answer the message, document a published correction or meet a legal obligation. We then delete or anonymize it. You can ask what information we still have, request a correction or ask us to delete the thread by writing to desk@northdesk.ca. We may need to verify that the request comes from the email account concerned.

Other sites

Articles link to Job Bank, Statistics Canada, the Canada Revenue Agency and provincial pages. Those sites have their own notices. We do not control them.
