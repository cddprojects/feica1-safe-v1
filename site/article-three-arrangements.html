Online, remote and flexible are three different agreements

North Desk · Last reviewed October 2026

Search boxes treat the three words as synonyms. Contracts do not.

Keep them apart

  • Remote is a place rule.
  • Online is how the work travels.
  • Flexible is an hours or load rule.
  • A role can be one of these, or all three. The ad should say which.

Place

Remote work is not performed at the employer’s office. Hybrid work is partly there. If the message says remote and then names three office days, believe the office days. Guides: remote.

Channel

Online work is delivered over the internet. It can be remote, or it can be a seat in a building with a headset. When a pitch says only “online job,” ask for the place rule in writing. Guide: online.

Hours

Flexible work lets the week move. Statistics Canada’s part-time line is usually under 30 hours at the main job. A flexible arrangement can still be full-time, and a short week can still be poorly paid. Guide: flexible.

Why the mix-up matters in recruitment

Unclear ads attract the wrong people, and vague pitches borrow all three words at once: online, remote, flexible, high daily pay, no experience. A recruitable role can say which of the three it is in a sentence. If it cannot, you do not yet have a role. You have a description. Read the field order next.
