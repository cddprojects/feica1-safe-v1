Work types

Remote, online and flexible work.

Remote describes location. Online describes how work is completed. Part-time and flexible describe hours or workload.

01 · Location

Remote

Remote jobs are completed away from the employer’s workplace. Some allow work from anywhere in Canada, while others are limited to a province. Hybrid jobs include on-site days. Job Bank identifies remote positions with a work-location filter.

Read the guide

02 · How work is done

Online

Online work is completed or delivered over the internet, including customer support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, moderation and software development. It may be done at home or at an employer’s workplace.

Read the guide

03 · Hours

Flexible

Flexible work provides some choice over schedule, hours or workload. Part-time work describes hours and may still have a fixed schedule. Project work may be flexible, but deadlines and availability requirements still matter.

Read the guide

Once the arrangement is clear, Job Bank can filter an occupation by work location or hours.
