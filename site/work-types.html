Work types

Choose the flexibility that matters to you.

Work from home changes the place. Online work describes what can be delivered over the internet. Part-time and flexible work change the shape of the week.

01 · Place

Remote

You are not required to sit in the employer’s office. The ad should say whether that means anywhere in a province, anywhere in Canada, or a hybrid split. Job Bank files this under a remote filter, separate from a keyword search.

Read the guide

02 · Work

Online

The tasks and completed work move over the internet: support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, moderation or software. Online does not always mean work from home. Read where the posting says the work must be done.

Read the guide

03 · Hours

Flexible

The week can move: chosen hours, a project deadline, or a part-time load. Statistics Canada counts part-time as usually under 30 hours a week. Flexible is not a promise of income, and it is not permission to skip the contract.

Read the guide

Current openings, when you want them, are on Job Bank. Use the remote filter rather than the words “work from home” alone.
