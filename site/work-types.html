Work types

Three kinds of flexible work.

Remote work is about where you work. Online work is about how the work is done. Part-time and flexible work are about your hours.

01 · Location

Remote

You are not normally required to work at the employer’s workplace. The job posting should state whether you may work anywhere in a province, anywhere in Canada or under a hybrid arrangement. Job Bank identifies these positions through its remote-work filter.

Read the guide

02 · Work

Online

The tasks are completed and delivered online: customer support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, moderation or software development. Online does not always mean working from home. Check where the posting says the work must be done.

Read the guide

03 · Hours

Flexible

The schedule may include flexible hours, deadline-based projects or part-time work. Statistics Canada defines part-time work as usually fewer than 30 hours a week. Flexible work does not guarantee income, and you should still review the employment agreement or contract.

Read the guide

For current job postings, visit Job Bank. Use the remote-work filter instead of relying only on the phrase “work from home.”
