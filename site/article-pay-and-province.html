Pay

What a second paycheque changes

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

A second job can add income, but there are details to check: the province or territory tied to the work, the applicable minimum wage and how deductions are handled.

The short version

  • There is no single minimum wage for every remote job in Canada. Check the federal rate and the province or territory named in the posting.
  • Banks, airlines, federally regulated telecommunications companies and interprovincial transportation businesses follow the Canada Labour Code.
  • Job Bank says a remote posting follows the wage rules of the employer’s province, which can differ from yours.
  • A second employer still deducts tax and Canada Pension Plan contributions. Follow current CRA instructions when completing Form TD1.

Minimum wage for remote work

Minimum wage is set province by province, with a separate federal rate for federally regulated employees. From 1 April 2026 the federal rate is $18.15 an hour. If the provincial general rate is higher, a federally regulated employer must meet the higher one. Alberta’s general rate is still $15. British Columbia’s is $18.25 from 1 June 2026. Ontario’s is $17.95 from 1 October 2026. Quebec’s is $16.60 from 1 May 2026. Confirm the current figure on the federal minimum wage database and on the province’s own site before you rely on a list, including ours.

Job Bank tells searchers that remote jobs follow the wage regulations of the province where the employer is located, and that those can differ from the rules where you live. Read the province in the posting, then confirm the applicable rules. This page does not determine which law applies to your contract.

What job postings must show in two provinces

British Columbia has required pay information in covered public job postings since 1 November 2023. Ontario’s rules from 1 January 2026 require many employers to show expected compensation or a range, disclose artificial-intelligence screening and say whether a vacancy exists. Details and exceptions are in How to read a remote job posting. The posted range is not yet your offer. Quebec employment standards are administered by CNESST, while workplace language requirements are governed under the Charter of the French Language and overseen by the OQLF.

A second paycheque

If you already have an employer, a second employer still deducts income tax from your pay. If you have already claimed personal tax credits on another TD1 and your total income will exceed those credits, you generally enter zero and check the appropriate box on the second TD1. Follow current CRA instructions for your situation.

Separate employers generally calculate CPP deductions independently. If the two jobs together result in contributions above the annual maximum, the extra employee contribution may be refunded or applied to tax owing when you file. The line for that overpayment is 44800. Quebec uses the Quebec Pension Plan, and the process is different. Rates change every year, so check the current CRA guidance.

A contract that calls you freelance does not determine whether you are an employee. The Canada Revenue Agency considers control, tools, subcontracting, financial risk and opportunity for profit. Job Compass cannot determine your employment status. This is not tax advice.
