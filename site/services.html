Why it matters

Why people look for extra work.

Additional work can help with everyday costs, build experience or provide hours that fit around a main job, school or family responsibilities.

Everyone has a reason

Why people take on more work.

In August 2023, Statistics Canada asked people with more than one job for their main reason. Income was the main reason for many respondents, but not for everyone.

Help with everyday costs

Paying for essentials like groceries or housing was the top reason.

Add income

Nearly as many wanted money beyond what their main job pays.

Build experience

Some wanted to do work they care about, and a second job can be a practical way to get experience in a new field.

Find hours that fit

Part-time or flexible work can fit around a main job, school, family or health needs.

Source: Statistics Canada, August 2023, people aged 15 to 69 who already had more than one job. These results describe current multiple jobholders rather than the outcome of a new job search. See the full numbers.

What changes with each type

Where, how and when you work.

Remote work can cut or remove the commute, though some jobs still need you to live in a certain province or work in a certain time zone.

Online work is completed or delivered over the internet, but it is not necessarily remote or flexible.

Flexible work can mean a shorter week or more say over your hours. Even so, find out who sets the schedule, how much work there is and how you get paid.

About Job Compass

Canadian sources in one place.

Information about remote work is spread across job postings, Job Bank, Statistics Canada, the Canada Revenue Agency and provincial websites.

Job Compass brings those sources together in guides on work arrangements, job searches, applications, pay and employment status.

Job Compass is an independent publisher. See the About page for our editorial approach.

More than a paycheque

Practical needs and personal goals both count.

Additional work can take the form of a second job that supports household costs, a freelance project that builds experience or a flexible position that fits an existing schedule.

The information is for people already considering additional work and comparing their options.
