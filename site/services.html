Why it matters

Why people look for extra work.

For some it helps with everyday costs. For others it is a way to build experience or find hours that fit around a main job, school or family. Knowing which applies to you makes the search easier.

Start with your reason

People have different reasons.

Statistics Canada asked people who already held more than one job about their main reason in August 2023. Their answers show that income matters, but it is not the whole story.

Help with everyday costs

Paying for essentials such as groceries or housing was the most common main reason.

Add income

Nearly as many people wanted income beyond what their main job provided.

Build experience

Some wanted to do work they were passionate about. A second job can also be a practical way to develop experience in a field.

Find hours that fit

Part-time or flexible work may fit around a main job, studies, family responsibilities or health needs.

Source: Statistics Canada, August 2023, people aged 15 to 69 who already held more than one job. The results do not predict what a new job search will provide. See the full figures.

What these arrangements can change

Where, how and when you work.

Remote work can reduce or remove a commute, but a position may still require you to live in a particular province or work in a stated time zone.

Online work can make some tasks available from a computer, but online does not always mean remote or flexible.

Flexible work may offer a shorter schedule or some choice over hours. The posting should still explain who sets the schedule, how much work is available and how you will be paid.

The idea behind Job Compass

Why Job Compass exists.

Information about remote work is spread across job postings, Job Bank, Statistics Canada, the Canada Revenue Agency and provincial or territorial websites. It can be difficult to know which details matter to your situation.

Job Compass puts that information in one place. Our guides help you choose a work arrangement, narrow a search, read a job posting, prepare a resume and understand basic questions about pay and employment status.

We provide independent guidance. We do not publish jobs, place candidates or promise a particular income.

More than a paycheque

Practical needs and personal goals can both matter.

Taking on more work does not have to mean leaving your main job or finding a perfect role. It can be a second job that supports a household need, a freelance project that builds experience or a flexible position that fits your schedule.

The goal is not to tell every reader that they need another job. It is to help people who are already considering one understand their options, limits and next steps.
