From interest to a useful search

Begin with what you need—not every opening.

Remote work, online work and freelance projects can look similar in a search result. A few choices make it easier to recognise the arrangement that belongs in your life.

  1. Start with the change you want: more room in the budget, a different field, fewer hours or less travel.
  2. Set an honest amount of time for a part-time role or side job.
  3. Decide whether you need fully remote work or simply want some work-from-home days.
  4. Search Job Bank by occupation, then use the remote and hours filters.
  5. Before you apply, check the employer, pay, province and whether the work is employment or freelance.
