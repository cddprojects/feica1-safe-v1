Recruitment, explained

What a careful remote hire looks like.

Recruitment for this work is still a sequence: a real organisation, a written role, a conversation, then an offer. North Desk publishes that sequence so you can compare it with the message in your inbox.

  1. You apply on the employer’s own site or on a board such as Job Bank. You do not pay to be considered.
  2. Someone who works at that organisation interviews you. The equipment, hours, time zone and place are in writing.
  3. Pay is stated as an amount or a range. In Ontario and British Columbia, many public ads must show it.
  4. You can decline. Nothing about your bank account, crypto wallet or a private app is required in order to “unlock” the job.
