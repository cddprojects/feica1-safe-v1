From interest to a useful search

Begin with what you need—not every opening.

Remote work, online work and freelance projects can look similar in search results. A few choices make it easier to recognize the work arrangement that fits your needs.

  1. Start with the change you want: more room in the budget, a different field, fewer hours or less travel.
  2. Decide how many hours you can realistically work in a part-time job or side job.
  3. Decide whether you need fully remote work or simply want some work-from-home days.
  4. Search Job Bank by occupation, then use the remote and hours filters.
  5. Before you apply, check the employer, pay, province and whether you would be an employee or an independent contractor.
