Why people look

Three reasons, from people who already had two jobs.

  1. 34.9%

    Essentials

    Paying for groceries, housing and other needs. This was the largest reason in August 2023.

  2. 34.8%

    Extra income

    Money beyond the basics. Almost the same share named this as the main reason.

  3. 14.5%

    The work itself

    A field they were passionate about. A second dream is sometimes the occupation, not only the pay.

  4. Hours

    A week that fits

    School, children, health, or a shorter week. Flexible hours are how that sits beside everything else.

The three percentages are from Statistics Canada’s Labour Force Survey supplement, August 2023, workers aged 15 to 69 who held more than one job. In 2025 the annual count of multiple jobholders was 1,178,600. Figures are dated again in the article.

How to begin

Five decisions, then a search.

A second path is easier to find when you can say what it is for. North Desk does not match you to an employer. These are the questions the guides answer.

  1. Name the reason: essential costs, extra income, a field you want, or hours that fit.
  2. Write down the hours you can give in a normal week, beside the job or care you already have.
  3. Decide the place: fully remote, or online work that still has a city attached.
  4. Search Job Bank with the remote filter and an hours filter. Save it as an alert.
  5. Read who employs you, the pay, and whether you would be an employee or a contractor.
