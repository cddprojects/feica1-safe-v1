Searching for work

Finding suitable remote and online opportunities.

Relevant results depend on more than a “work from home” keyword. A practical search begins with an occupation, narrows results by work location and hours, and then checks each posting for the employer, pay and employment or contract status.

  1. 1

    Reason for searching

    Extra income, experience, fewer hours or less travel.

  2. 2

    Available hours

    The number of hours that can fit around current commitments.

  3. 3

    Work location

    Fully remote work and hybrid work lead to different results.

  4. 4

    Occupation

    Job Bank can filter an occupation by work location and hours.

  5. 5

    Posting details

    The employer, pay, province and employment or contract status.

How to search for remote work

Canadian job market

Remote and flexible work in Canada’s job market.

Remote openings form a small share of the market. Suitable opportunities depend on occupation, province, experience, available hours and the terms of the posting.

Roles and employers

Occupation and arrangement are separate details

Customer support, administration, software, writing, design and specialist services may be delivered online, depending on the employer and the work involved.

Timing and sources

Postings change throughout the search

Job Bank alerts identify new matches. Employer career pages confirm current vacancies, while Job Bank trend data provides regional context.

Requirements and competition

Role requirements still apply

Qualifications, location, communication, tools and time-zone availability still matter. Response times vary with each employer’s process.

In October 2026, about 700 of roughly 84,000 Job Bank postings were marked remote. Counts change as employers add and close postings.
