Work arrangements

Flexible work comes in more than one form.

Remote describes location. Online describes how work is delivered. Part-time and flexible describe hours or workload. A single posting may combine several of them.

01 · Location

Remote work

The work is completed away from the employer’s workplace. A remote position may still be limited to a province, territory or time zone. Hybrid work includes required on-site days.

About remote work

02 · Delivery

Online work

The work is completed or delivered over the internet. Online work can be remote, hybrid or on-site, and may include customer support, writing, bookkeeping, tutoring, design or software work.

About online work

03 · Hours

Part-time and flexible work

Part-time describes the number of hours. Flexible describes some choice over schedule or workload. A part-time job can have fixed hours, and a flexible job can still be full-time.

About flexible work

04 · Relationship

Freelance and additional work

Freelance usually describes independent contract work. A second job describes work held alongside another job and may be regular employment or contract work.

A focused job search

A better-matched search starts with the right details.

Occupation, work location, schedule and employment status determine which postings are relevant. Together, these five details narrow a broad search.

  1. 1

    Occupation

    A job title or occupation produces more relevant results than searching only for “work from home.”

  2. 2

    Work location

    Remote, hybrid and on-site labels show where the work is completed and whether attendance is required.

  3. 3

    Hours and availability

    Weekly hours, time zone and required availability determine whether the schedule fits.

  4. 4

    Working relationship

    Employee and independent-contractor arrangements handle control, expenses, deductions and taxes differently.

  5. 5

    Posting details

    A useful posting identifies the employer, location, duties, pay basis and application method.

Canadian context

A second job can be about more than extra income.

For some Canadians, additional work covers everyday costs. For others, it creates extra income or a chance to do meaningful work. Statistics Canada measured these reasons in August 2023.

  1. 34.9%

    Everyday needs

    Rent, groceries and other everyday costs.

  2. 34.8%

    Extra income

    Almost the same share wanted money beyond the basics.

  3. 14.5%

    Work they cared about

    This group cited work they were passionate about.

Source: Statistics Canada, August 2023. Read why people take a second job.
