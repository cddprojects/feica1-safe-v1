Remote work in Canada: statistics and search filters

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

Work-from-home statistics describe employed people, not current remote vacancies. Job Bank filters organize postings by occupation and work location.

In May 2026, 11.4 per cent of employed Canadians worked exclusively from home and 9.8 per cent had a hybrid arrangement. These figures describe employed people, not current remote vacancies.

Why the remote filter matters

Statistics on people working from home do not count open jobs. Current postings can be searched by occupation and Job Bank’s work-location filter. The remote-work search guide covers the steps.

Part-time remote work

Part-time and remote are separate Job Bank filters. Combining them narrows the results, while a saved search or alert can track new matches.

Sources

  • Statistics Canada: Labour Force Survey, May 2026, work location
