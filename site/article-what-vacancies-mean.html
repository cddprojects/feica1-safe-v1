What to expect when you search for remote work

North Desk · Last reviewed October 2026

Home work is a normal part of employment in Canada. A remote filter on a job board is a shorter list than the national vacancy total. Use both facts to set the search, not to talk yourself out of it.

2.8%vacancy rate, unchanged on the year
3.0unemployed people for every vacancy
6.5%unemployment rate, September 2026

Start from the arrangement, not the national total

Statistics Canada’s Job Vacancy and Wage Survey counted 510,200 vacancies in the second quarter of 2026. The vacancy rate was 2.8 per cent. There were 3.0 unemployed people for every vacancy. In September 2026 the Labour Force Survey put unemployment at 6.5 per cent. Those numbers mix offices, shops, trucks, kitchens and clinics. They are the background, not the size of a remote search.

A more useful figure for this desk is where people already work. In May 2026, 11.4 per cent of employed Canadians worked only from home, and 9.8 per cent had a hybrid week. Exclusive home work was lower than a year earlier. It is still a large group of real jobs. A second path is usually a slice of that: fewer hours, online delivery, or both.

Search the filter, not the whole board

Job Bank’s home page, when we read it in October 2026, invited people to search on the order of 84,000 postings. A browse the same month showed on the order of 700 marked remote. Both counts move every day. The practical reading is that a title plus the remote filter is a better search than opening the whole board. The words “work from home” are looser still and return hybrid and on-site ads. The steps are in where to look.

One occupation that can be done online

Software developers and programmers had 3,545 vacancies in that quarter, up about 35 per cent on the year, with an average offered hourly wage of $43.65. Offered wages are what employers advertised for empty jobs in that occupation. They are not the going rate for every remote role, and they are not an offer. Use an occupation you can already do, then read the pay on that posting.

How to use this when you are adding hours

Expect a specific search to take time. A part-time remote role is a narrower ask than “any job.” That is a reason to save a Job Bank alert, not a reason to treat a national vacancy total as your chance of being hired. North Desk does not post the ads.
