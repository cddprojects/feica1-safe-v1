What the remote job market looks like in Canada

North Desk · Last reviewed October 2026

Working from home is an established part of Canada’s labour market. The number of remote postings is smaller than the country’s total job count, so a focused search matters.

510,200job vacancies, all industries, second quarter of 2026
2.8%vacancy rate, unchanged on the year
3.0unemployed people for every vacancy
6.5%unemployment rate, September 2026

Start from the arrangement, not the national total

Statistics Canada’s Job Vacancy and Wage Survey counted 510,200 vacancies in the second quarter of 2026. The vacancy rate was 2.8 per cent. There were 3.0 unemployed people for every vacancy. In September 2026 the Labour Force Survey put unemployment at 6.5 per cent. Those numbers mix offices, shops, trucks, kitchens and clinics. They are the background, not the size of a remote search.

Work-location data provide more useful context for a remote search. In May 2026, 11.4 per cent of employed Canadians worked exclusively from home, and 9.8 per cent had a hybrid arrangement. Working exclusively from home was less common than a year earlier, but it remained an established work arrangement. People looking for a second job may narrow that group further by seeking fewer hours, online delivery or both.

Search the filter, not the whole board

When we reviewed Job Bank in October 2026, its home page showed approximately 84,000 job postings, while approximately 700 were marked remote. Both counts change daily. Searching for a job title and applying the remote filter is more useful than browsing all postings. Searching only for “work from home” is less precise and can return hybrid and on-site positions. Follow the steps in our guide to finding remote work.

One occupation that can be done online

Software developers and programmers had 3,545 vacancies in that quarter, up about 35 per cent year over year, with an average offered hourly wage of $43.65. Offered wages are the amounts employers advertised for vacant positions in that occupation. They are not the typical wage for every remote position, and they are not an offer. Search for an occupation for which you are qualified, then review the pay in each posting.

How to use this when you are adding hours

A focused search may take time. Part-time remote work is a narrower search than “any job.” Save a Job Bank alert rather than treating the national vacancy total as your likelihood of being hired. North Desk does not publish job postings.
