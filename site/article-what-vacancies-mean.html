The market

What Canada’s vacancy numbers do not say about remote work

North Desk · Last reviewed October 2026

The national vacancy total is real. It is also the wrong headline for a remote search.

510,200job vacancies, all industries, second quarter of 2026
2.8%vacancy rate, unchanged on the year
3.0unemployed people for every vacancy
6.5%unemployment rate, September 2026

The survey is the whole economy

Statistics Canada’s Job Vacancy and Wage Survey counted 510,200 vacancies in the second quarter of 2026. The vacancy rate was 2.8 per cent. There were 3.0 unemployed people for every vacancy. In September 2026 the Labour Force Survey put unemployment at 6.5 per cent, after employment fell by 68,000 in the month. The share of people who had been unemployed in August and had a job in September was 30.6 per cent, below the 2017–2019 average of 36.5 per cent.

Those numbers describe offices, shops, trucks, kitchens, clinics and job sites together. They do not describe a remote market. Health care, construction, trucking, retail and food service dominate what Job Bank calls in demand. Most of that work is on a floor, a ward or a road. North Desk does not recruit for it and does not write guides for it.

Remote is a thin slice of the board

Job Bank’s home page, when we read it in October 2026, invited people to search on the order of 84,000 postings. A browse page the same month showed on the order of 700 marked remote. Both counts move every day. The useful point is the ratio, not the exact tally: remote is a filter inside a much larger board, not the board itself.

A search for the words “work from home” is looser still. It returns hybrid and on-site ads. Use the remote filter if remote is the condition you need.

One occupation that can be done online

Software developers and programmers had 3,545 vacancies in that quarter, up about 35 per cent on the year, with an average offered hourly wage of $43.65. Offered wages are what employers advertised for empty jobs. They are lower, in the aggregate, than the $37.64 average hourly wage of people already employed in September. Do not read $43.65 as the going remote rate in Canada.

How to use the numbers

Use them to stay calm. A cooler national market means a remote application can take time, and a message promising fast daily pay is not “the market.” Use Job Bank when you want ads. Use these articles when you want to know what an ad is allowed to mean.
