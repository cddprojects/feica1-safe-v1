A publisher, not an agency

Job Compass publishes general information about online, remote and flexible work in Canada, and how employers hire for that work. We are not a licensed employment agency. We do not publish job postings, hire, screen, place or introduce candidates. We do not charge readers a fee.

No guarantee of employment or income

Nothing on this site is an offer of employment or a prediction of earnings. Pay, hours and eligibility depend on the employer, the applicable jurisdiction and the terms of the working arrangement.

Not professional advice

The pages are not legal, tax, immigration or employment advice. Most employment standards are provincial or territorial. Federally regulated workplaces follow the Canada Labour Code. Quebec has distinct employment and workplace-language requirements that should be checked with the appropriate authorities. Confirm details with the authority that governs your situation or with a qualified adviser.

Figures go out of date

We date statistics and identify their sources. Vacancy counts, minimum wages and job-posting rules can change after an article’s review date. Check the linked official source for current information and contact us if you find an error.

Other websites

Links to Job Bank, Statistics Canada and other sites are there so you can read the source. We are not responsible for their content.
