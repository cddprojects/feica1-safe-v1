Disclaimer

A publisher, not an agency

North Desk publishes general information about online, remote and flexible work in Canada, and about how recruitment for that work usually proceeds. We are not a licensed employment agency. We do not publish vacancies. We do not recruit, screen, place or introduce candidates. We do not charge a fee to readers.

No job and no income

Nothing on this site is an offer of employment or a prediction of earnings. Pay, hours and eligibility change by employer, province and contract.

Not professional advice

The pages are not legal, tax, immigration or employment advice. Employment standards are provincial, with a federal code for some industries. Quebec’s rules, including French-language requirements at work, are not restated as if they were Ontario’s. Confirm details with the authority that governs your situation, or with a qualified adviser.

Figures go out of date

We date statistics and say where they came from. Vacancy counts, minimum wages and posting rules change. A reviewed date is not a promise that every sentence is still current. Write to us if you find an error.

Other websites

Links to Job Bank, Statistics Canada and other sites are there so you can read the source. We are not responsible for their content.
