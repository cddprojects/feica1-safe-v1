Scope and limitations

Disclaimer

A publisher, not an agency

Job Compass publishes general information about online, remote and flexible work in Canada and how employers hire for that work. It is not a licensed employment agency and does not publish job postings, hire, screen, place or introduce candidates. Readers are not charged a fee.

No guarantee of employment or income

Nothing on this site is a job offer or a promise of earnings. Pay, hours and eligibility depend on the employer, where the rules apply and the terms of the job.

Not professional advice

The pages provide general information, not legal, tax, immigration or employment advice. Most employment standards are provincial or territorial. Federally regulated workplaces follow the Canada Labour Code. Quebec has distinct employment and workplace-language requirements. Advice for an individual situation should come from the responsible authority or a qualified adviser.

Figures go out of date

Statistics are dated and linked to their sources. Vacancy counts, minimum wages and posting rules can change after a guide is reviewed. Current figures remain available from the linked source, and possible errors can be reported through the contact page.

Other websites

Links to government and other websites provide access to original sources. Job Compass is not responsible for third-party content.
