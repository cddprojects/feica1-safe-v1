Second jobs

Why people take a second job

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

A second job or side income can help with household costs, build experience in a field you care about or fit around the rest of your life.

What people told Statistics Canada

  • 34.9 per cent: to pay for essentials like groceries or housing.
  • 34.8 per cent: to earn extra income.
  • 14.5 per cent: to do work they cared about.
  • Hours and location decide whether a second job can fit beside the first.

How common is multiple jobholding?

In 2023, 5.6 per cent of employed Canadians held more than one job at the same time, about 1.1 million people. Women were more likely to do so than men (6.4 per cent, compared with 4.8 per cent). In 2025, Statistics Canada’s annual count of multiple jobholders was 1,178,600. For some, one job does not cover household costs. Others use a second job to pursue a different kind of work.

Those August 2023 results come from a Statistics Canada Labour Force Survey supplement of workers aged 15 to 69 who already held more than one job.

Why online, remote and flexible work suit a second job

Most people who held two jobs in 2023 also held at least one part-time job: 89.9 per cent. A second commute can make the schedule impossible. Remote work skips that trip, and flexible hours can work around another shift, school or family. Online work can include customer support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring and software development.

Home is already a workplace for a minority of employed Canadians. In May 2026, 11.4 per cent worked only from home, and 9.8 per cent split the week between home and another place. Those figures cover the whole economy rather than available remote jobs. They show that working from home is an established work arrangement in Canada.

Different goals lead to different searches

If you need to cover essentials or bring in more money, begin with the hours you can really give and the kind of pay you need. Regular employee pay is usually steadier than contract income, which can swing from month to month. If you want to build experience, start with skills you can already show, and have a look at our resume guide. If work has to fit around school, caregiving or health, read about flexible hours before you go after a full-time remote job.

The guide to searching for remote work covers Job Bank’s remote and hours filters. The search goals page groups guides by common reasons for seeking additional work.
