Glossary

Words the ads use.

Short definitions for online, remote and flexible recruitment in Canada. Confirm anything with a legal consequence on the official page linked from the article.

Remote
The work is not performed at the employer’s office. It can still require you to live in a named province or time zone.
Hybrid
Some of the work is remote and some is at a stated place. It is not fully remote.
On-site
You attend a workplace. North Desk does not cover these roles.
Online work
The tasks and the deliverable move over the internet. The word does not, by itself, set the place or the hours.
Flexible work
Hours or workload can move. On this site, only when the work is also online or remote.
Part-time
In the Labour Force Survey, usually under 30 hours a week at the main or only job.
Job Bank
The Government of Canada’s public job board. Use the remote filter when place is the point.
NOC
National Occupational Classification. A code for an occupation, used on Job Bank and in labour statistics.
Vacancy
A job an employer is trying to fill. Statistics Canada measures vacancies by survey. A posting on a board is not the same count.
Offered wage
The wage advertised for an empty job. It is not the average wage of people already employed, and it is not your offer.
CPP
Canada Pension Plan. Contributions can apply to employment, including part-time employment. See the Canada Revenue Agency for current rates.
EI
Employment Insurance. Premiums and benefits have their own rules. A contract that calls you freelance does not automatically decide them.
Independent contractor
A worker who is in business for themselves. CRA looks at control, tools, risk and chance of profit, not only the contract’s title.
LMIA
A Labour Market Impact Assessment. An employer process used in some temporary-foreign-worker hires. It is not a job you buy. A request to pay someone to arrange one is a warning sign.
Gig
Short contracts, casual hours or platform tasks. Flexibility is real. A wage is not guaranteed, and platform delivery in B.C. has its own minimum for engaged time.

No term matches that.
