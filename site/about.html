About Job Compass

Canadian information on remote, online and flexible work, grounded in public sources and common job-search needs.

Who the guides are for

These guides are for people in Canada deciding whether remote, online, part-time or flexible work suits their circumstances. They cover work location, hours, job postings, pay and employment status, including questions that arise when work must fit around another job, school, caregiving or health needs.

Mission

To make Canadian job-market information easier to understand, so readers can identify suitable work arrangements and recognize important terms before applying.

Vision

A job search in which people can distinguish work arrangements, assess postings with realistic expectations and know when an official source is needed.

Why this information matters

Work-arrangement labels can hide differences in location, hours, pay and employment status. Clear explanations give job seekers a stronger basis for deciding whether a posting suits their circumstances.

Job Compass is an independent Canadian publisher. It is not connected to any federal, provincial or territorial government, is not a licensed employment agency and does not send reader information to employers.

Defined scope

Coverage focuses on remote, online, part-time and flexible arrangements. Fully on-site positions are outside the editorial scope.

Backed by sources

Labour figures come from Statistics Canada, while pay and tax information comes from the Canada Revenue Agency and the appropriate provincial, territorial or federal authority.

Dated and reviewed

Each guide shows when it was last reviewed, and wage and vacancy figures say which period they cover.

Job Compass publishes general information rather than legal, tax or employment advice. Contact: hello@jobcompass.ca. Job Compass operates online and has no public office.
