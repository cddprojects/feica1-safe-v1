About

We publish on one kind of hiring.

Who is writing

North Desk is an independent publisher for people in Canada who are looking at online work, remote roles, or flexible hours attached to that work. Recruitment is the subject: how a real hiring process runs, how an ad is built, and how a pitch imitates one. We are not affiliated with Job Bank, Statistics Canada, or any province.

We are not a licensed employment agency. We do not hold jobs, and we do not send your name to an employer.

How a piece gets written

Inside the beat

If the work is not online, remote, or flexible in that setting, we do not assign it.

Tied to a source

Labour figures come from Statistics Canada. Posting rules come from the province that wrote them. Scam patterns come from the Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre.

Dated

Articles say when they were last reviewed. A wage or a vacancy count without a date is not finished.

North Desk publishes general information. It is not legal, tax or employment advice. Write to us at desk@northdesk.ca. We do not publish a street address because the desk is the site.
