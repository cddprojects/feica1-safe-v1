About Job Compass

Canadian information on remote, online and flexible work, grounded in public sources and practical job-search needs.

Who the guides are for

Job Compass is an independent Canadian publisher for people considering online work, a remote position or flexible hours around a main job, family or school. The guides explain how these arrangements differ, where paid opportunities are advertised and how hours, pay and employment status affect the choice. Job Compass is not connected to Job Bank, Statistics Canada or any provincial or territorial government.

Job Compass is not a licensed employment agency. It does not post job openings or send reader information to employers.

Editorial approach

Defined scope

Coverage focuses on remote, online, part-time and flexible arrangements. Fully on-site positions are outside the editorial scope.

Backed by sources

Labour figures come from Statistics Canada, search tips from Job Bank, and pay and tax information from the Canada Revenue Agency and the right provincial, territorial or federal authority.

Dated and reviewed

Each guide shows when it was last reviewed, and wage and vacancy figures say which period they cover.

Job Compass publishes general information rather than legal, tax or employment advice. Contact: hello@jobcompass.ca. Job Compass operates online and has no public office.
