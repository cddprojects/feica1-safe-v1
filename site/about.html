About Job Compass

Independent information about work in Canada

Job Compass is an editorial publisher covering remote, online and flexible work in Canada. The site is for readers comparing work arrangements, searching for reliable job information or reviewing the terms in a posting.

Job Compass publishes guides rather than job listings. It is not affiliated with Job Bank, Statistics Canada, the Canada Revenue Agency or any federal, provincial or territorial government.

Topics covered

  • Remote, hybrid, online, part-time and flexible work
  • Job Bank search filters and employer career pages
  • Job postings, resumes and written offers
  • Pay, payroll deductions and employee or contractor status
