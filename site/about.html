About North Desk

We cover online, remote and flexible work

Who we write for

North Desk is an independent publisher for people in Canada who are considering online work, a remote position or flexible hours alongside a main job, family responsibilities or studies. We explain why people take on this work and how to look for it on Job Bank and employers’ websites. We are not affiliated with Job Bank, Statistics Canada or any provincial or territorial government.

We are not a licensed employment agency. We do not publish job openings or send your information to employers.

How we prepare an article

Within our scope

We cover online, remote and flexible work. On-site positions are outside our editorial focus.

Based on sources

Labour figures come from Statistics Canada. Job-search guidance comes from Job Bank. Pay and tax information comes from the Canada Revenue Agency and the relevant provincial, territorial or federal authority.

Reviewed and dated

Articles state when they were last reviewed. Wage and vacancy figures include the period they describe.

North Desk publishes general information, not legal, tax or employment advice. Contact us at desk@northdesk.ca. We operate online and do not have a public office.
