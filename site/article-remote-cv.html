Tailor your resume for online and remote work

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

A resume for remote or online work can make relevant results, communication experience and tools easy to identify.

Relevant resume information

  • Results you delivered on your own.
  • Examples of work completed with limited supervision.
  • Relevant communication, collaboration and project tools.
  • Your city and province when location eligibility matters.

What employers look for

For online and remote jobs, employers want to know you can get the work done reliably without close supervision. For each relevant job, say what you achieved: customer requests resolved, reports delivered, classes taught, software releases maintained. Name the communication and project tools you used. If your availability matters, mention it in your application or cover letter instead of the top of your resume.

Information not needed in an application

Use the job title you held and describe relevant results. Do not include a social insurance number, identification or banking details in an application. Your city and province are sufficient.

Resume review

Results are easier to assess than copied duty lists. Concise examples can include requests resolved, reports delivered, classes taught or software releases maintained. The search guide covers where to find postings.
