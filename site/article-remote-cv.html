Application

Résumés for online and remote work

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

For remote and online roles, employers often assess prior results, written communication and the capacity to work with limited on-site supervision.

Relevant resume information

  • Results you delivered on your own.
  • The tools you use, named plainly.
  • A short summary at the top.
  • Your city and province, including any geographic work restrictions.

What employers look for

For online and remote roles, employers often look for reliable work completed with limited supervision. Each relevant role normally states outcomes, such as requests resolved, reports delivered, classes taught or releases maintained, and names the communication and project tools used. Availability is normally better placed in an application or cover letter than at the top of a résumé.

Information not needed in an application

The job title and relevant results are sufficient. Social insurance numbers, identification and banking details do not belong in an initial application. City and province usually provide enough location information.

Resume review

A skim-read résumé should surface outcomes and tools rather than duty lists alone. Applications are submitted on the employer’s site or through a job board. Search options are listed in How to search for remote work.
