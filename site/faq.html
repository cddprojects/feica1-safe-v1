FAQ

Questions about remote and flexible work

Does Job Compass list jobs?

No. Job Compass publishes guides to online, remote and flexible work and explains how hiring works. Current openings are available through Job Bank and employers’ career pages.

Is Job Compass a recruitment agency?

No. Job Compass is an independent publisher, not a licensed employment agency. It does not screen, place or introduce candidates, and it does not charge readers.

Why only online, remote and flexible work?

These arrangements can be easier to fit around a main job, school or family responsibilities. Job Compass covers work completed online or away from the employer’s office, including part-time and flexible arrangements.

Why do people take a second job?

In August 2023, Canadians with more than one job most often cited essentials (34.9 per cent), extra income (34.8 per cent) or work they cared about (14.5 per cent). Why people take a second job provides the full figures. The search goals page groups related guides by reason.

Is remote work common in Canada?

In May 2026, 11.4 per cent of employed Canadians worked only from home and 9.8 per cent had a hybrid week. Remote positions represent a small share of Job Bank postings. Searches are generally more useful when they combine an occupation or job title with the remote filter. Finding remote and online postings explains the available sources.

What is the difference between remote and online?

Remote describes where the work takes place. Online describes how the work is completed or delivered. A position can be one, the other or both. Remote, online and flexible work compares the arrangements.

Will I be paid a set amount?

A set amount applies only when it forms part of an employment or contract offer. Nothing on Job Compass is a job offer or a promise of earnings.

Which province’s rules apply if I work from home?

It depends on where you work, where the employer operates, the type of business and your employment relationship. Federally regulated workplaces follow federal labour standards. What a second paycheque changes has more, with official links.

Can this work be done in addition to a current job?

Additional work may be possible when the schedules are compatible and the existing employment agreement permits it. In 2023, 89.9 per cent of Canadians with two jobs had at least one part-time job. Employment agreements may also include conflict-of-interest requirements. The guides cover flexible hours and pay for a second job.

Is French content available for Quebec?

Not yet. These pages are in English. In Quebec, employment standards are handled by the CNESST, while workplace language requirements fall under the Charter of the French Language and the OQLF. Current information is available from those organizations.
