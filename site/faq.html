FAQ

Short answers.

Does North Desk list jobs?

No. We publish explanations of online, remote and flexible work, and of the recruitment process around that work. For current ads, use Job Bank’s remote filter and employers’ own careers pages.

Are you a recruitment agency?

No. We are an independent publisher. We are not a licensed employment agency. We do not screen, place or introduce candidates, and we do not charge job seekers.

Why only online, remote and flexible?

Because that is how a second job can exist beside a main job, school, or care at home. A second commute usually does not fit. On-site work, including trades, retail floors, trucking and bedside care, is outside these pages.

Why would someone want this as a second job?

In August 2023, people who already held more than one job most often named essentials (34.9 per cent), extra income (34.8 per cent), or work they were passionate about (14.5 per cent). The guide puts those figures in context. Which guide fits points you to the next page.

Is remote work common in Canada?

In May 2026, 11.4 per cent of employed people worked only from home, and 9.8 per cent had a hybrid week. On Job Bank, a remote filter is a much shorter list than every posting. Both are reasons to search with a title and a filter. Where to look.

What is the difference between remote and online?

Remote is where you are allowed to sit. Online is how the work travels. A job can be one, the other, or both. Read the three agreements.

Will I be paid a set amount?

Only if an employer offers you that amount in writing. Nothing on North Desk is an offer or a forecast of earnings.

Which province’s rules apply if I work from home?

For most employers, the rules of the province or territory where you do the work. Federally regulated employers, such as banks, airlines and telecoms, follow the Canada Labour Code. What a second paycheque changes has the detail and the links.

Can this sit beside the job I already have?

Often, if the hours are short enough and the place is remote. In 2023, 89.9 per cent of people with two jobs held at least one part-time job. Read flexible hours and pay and a second job before you treat two full-time roles as the plan.

Do you cover Quebec in French?

These pages are in English. Quebec employment standards and French-language workplace rules are administered by CNESST. Confirm those on Quebec’s own sites. We do not translate them here.
