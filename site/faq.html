FAQ

Short answers.

Does North Desk list jobs?

No. We publish explanations of online, remote and flexible work, and how employers hire for that work. For current job postings, use Job Bank’s remote filter and employers’ own careers pages.

Are you a recruitment agency?

No. We are an independent publisher. We are not a licensed employment agency. We do not screen, place or introduce candidates, and we do not charge job seekers.

Why only online, remote and flexible work?

These arrangements may make it easier to fit a second job around a main job, school or family responsibilities. North Desk focuses on work that can be completed online or away from an employer’s regular workplace, with part-time or flexible hours. On-site work is outside our scope.

Why would someone want this as a second job?

In August 2023, people who already held more than one job most often named essentials (34.9 per cent), extra income (34.8 per cent) or work they were passionate about (14.5 per cent). The guide puts those figures in context. Find the right guide points you to relevant information.

Is remote work common in Canada?

In May 2026, 11.4 per cent of employed people worked only from home, and 9.8 per cent had a hybrid week. On Job Bank, a remote filter is a much shorter list than every posting. Both are reasons to search with a title and a filter. Where to look.

What is the difference between remote and online?

Remote describes where you may work. Online describes how the work is completed or delivered. A job can be one, the other or both. Compare the three arrangements.

Will I be paid a set amount?

Only if an employer offers you that amount in writing. Nothing on North Desk is an offer or a forecast of earnings.

Which province’s rules apply if I work from home?

The applicable jurisdiction can depend on where you work, where the employer operates, the nature of the business and the employment relationship. Federally regulated workplaces follow federal labour standards. What a second paycheque changes provides context and official links.

Can I do this in addition to my current job?

Possibly, if the hours and your current employment terms allow it. In 2023, 89.9 per cent of people with two jobs held at least one part-time job. Review the proposed schedule, your employment agreement and any conflict-of-interest rules. Read about flexible hours and pay for a second job.

Do you cover Quebec in French?

These pages are in English. Quebec employment standards are administered by CNESST. Workplace language requirements are governed under the Charter of the French Language and overseen by the OQLF. Check those organizations’ websites for current information.
