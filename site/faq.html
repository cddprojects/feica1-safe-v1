FAQ

Questions about remote and flexible work

How do remote, online and flexible work differ?

Remote describes location, online describes how work is completed or delivered, and flexible describes schedule or workload. A position can include one, two or all three. Compare the arrangements.

Why do people take a second job?

Common reasons include household costs, additional income, experience and work that fits other commitments. Why people take a second job provides the Statistics Canada figures.

Is remote work common in Canada?

In May 2026, 11.4 per cent of employed Canadians worked only from home and 9.8 per cent had a hybrid arrangement. These figures describe current workers rather than available vacancies. Read how to interpret the numbers.

Does advertised pay guarantee what I will earn?

No. A posting describes expected compensation; pay is established by the employer’s written offer or contract.

Which province’s rules apply if I work from home?

It depends on where you work, where the employer operates, the type of business and your employment relationship. Federally regulated workplaces follow federal labour standards. Pay and deductions for additional work has more, with official links.

Can a second job be combined with a current job?

Check the schedules, employment agreement, workplace policies and possible conflicts of interest before combining jobs. See the guides on flexible hours and pay and deductions.

Where can Quebec readers verify employment rules?

CNESST provides employment-standards information. The OQLF provides information about workplace-language requirements.
