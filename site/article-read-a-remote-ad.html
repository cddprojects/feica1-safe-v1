Before you apply

How to read a remote job posting

Job Compass · Last reviewed October 2026

Before an application, the employer, work location, pay basis, weekly hours and employment status should be identifiable.

What to look for

  • Employer name and a matching careers page on the employer’s website
  • Work location: remote, hybrid, on-site or province-specific remote
  • Pay basis: hourly or annual amount, range or “to be negotiated”
  • Weekly hours, time zone and employment or independent contract work
  • Required availability in relation to other commitments

What to expect in a job posting

Job Bank and many employer sites show the job title, employer name, location, pay basis, work arrangement and date posted. Pay may be hourly or annual. If the wage says “to be negotiated,” the posting does not provide an amount.

Remote postings normally include the permitted work location. “Remote, Canada” and “Remote, must live in British Columbia” describe different arrangements and different applicant pools.

Pay in the posting

From 1 November 2023, British Columbia’s Pay Transparency Act requires provincially regulated employers to include expected wage or salary information in publicly advertised job postings. For covered publicly advertised job postings in Ontario, rules in effect from 1 January 2026 require many employers with at least 25 employees to include expected compensation or a range, disclose whether they use artificial intelligence to screen applicants and say whether a vacancy exists. A posted range in Ontario cannot span more than $50,000 a year, and positions paying more than $200,000 a year are exempt. A range does not guarantee the amount in a written offer. Employers cannot require Canadian experience in those Ontario postings or related application forms.

Hours, time zone and on-site requirements

The posting should identify weekly hours, time zone, required availability and any on-site days. Advertised pay remains posting information until it appears in a written offer or contract. The guide on how to search for remote work covers where postings appear.

Sources

  • Ontario: requirements for publicly advertised job postings
