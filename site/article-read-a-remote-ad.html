Before you apply

Read a remote job posting with confidence

North Desk · Last reviewed October 2026

A few details can help you decide whether a position fits your needs. Start with the employer, then review the location, pay, hours and working arrangement.

Read in this order

  • Who is hiring, and can you find that name on a website you looked up yourself?
  • Where may the work be done: remote, hybrid, or a named province?
  • What is the pay: an amount, a range, or the phrase “to be negotiated”?
  • What are the hours, the time zone, and whether you would be an employee or a contractor?
  • Do those hours fit beside the job, study, or care you already have?

What to expect in a job posting

Job Bank and Indeed postings generally include a job title, employer, location, pay, work-location label and posting date. Pay may be listed by the hour or by the year. If the wage says “to be negotiated,” no amount has been provided.

A remote posting should also explain where applicants may live and work. “Remote — Canada” and “remote — must live in British Columbia” have different location requirements. Indeed tells employers not to use a nationwide location unless people anywhere in the country can do the work.

Pay in the posting

British Columbia has required compensation information in covered public job postings since 1 November 2023. For covered publicly advertised job postings in Ontario, rules in effect from 1 January 2026 require many employers with at least 25 employees to include expected compensation or a range, disclose whether they use artificial intelligence to screen applicants and say whether a vacancy exists. A posted range in Ontario cannot span more than $50,000 a year, and positions paying more than $200,000 a year are exempt. A range does not guarantee the amount you will be offered. Employers cannot require Canadian experience in those Ontario postings or related application forms.

Indeed’s hiring lab measured the Ontario change: pay appeared on 49 per cent of Ontario postings just before the rule, and on 71 per cent by June 2026. Most of those were ranges.

Confirm the hours for a second job

If this would be a second job, the weekly hours and time zone matter more than the word flexible. A pay range in a posting describes the position; it becomes your pay only if the employer includes that amount in an offer.
