Hours

Flexible online and remote work

Flexible work gives you some say in your hours, workload or deadlines. Here we look at flexible jobs that are also online or remote.

At a glance

Part-time
Under 30 hours a week, by Statistics Canada’s definition.
Shapes
Hours you choose, a shorter week or a project with a deadline.
Not included
On-site casual shifts, even short ones.
Pay
May be hourly, salaried or project-based.

Last reviewed October 2026.

Why hours matter when you add a second job

Flexible work can mean fewer hours, a say in when you work, or no commute at all. Where you work is covered in our remote work guide. This one is about hours and workload.

A second job beside a full-time one only works if the hours fit your week. In 2023, 89.9 per cent of multiple jobholders held at least one part-time job. People whose main job was already part-time were more than twice as likely to hold another job as people whose main job was full-time (9.7 per cent, compared with 4.7 per cent). Statistics Canada’s part-time line is usually under 30 hours a week at the main job. A second job can be much shorter than that. That line is just for statistics. It has nothing to do with overtime rules in your province.

Plenty of people work fewer hours because of school, caring for kids or family, health or simply preference. A good posting tells you the weekly hours or deadlines, the time zone and who sets the schedule. Our guide to reading a job posting covers what to look for.

Self-employment, which includes a lot of flexible online contract work, was up 51,000 people (+1.9 per cent) in the year to September 2026, and down 23,000 in that month alone. Contract income may vary from month to month and is generally less predictable than regular employee pay.

Asking your employer for flexible hours

Employees in federally regulated workplaces have a formal right to request flexible work arrangements. Everyone else is covered by provincial or territorial law, workplace policy and any collective agreement or contract. It helps to put your request in writing, describe the schedule you have in mind and keep the reply. Be careful with advice written for other countries, since the rules here are different.
