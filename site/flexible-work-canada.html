Hours

Flexible online and remote work

Flexible work gives you some say in your hours, workload or deadlines. Here we look at flexible jobs that are also online or remote.

At a glance

Part-time
Under 30 hours a week, by Statistics Canada’s definition.
Arrangements
Choice over start and finish times, workload or project deadlines.
Location
Flexible work may be remote, hybrid or on-site.
Pay
May be hourly, salaried or project-based.

Last reviewed October 2026.

What flexible work can mean

Flexible work can mean fewer hours, some control over start and finish times, or work organized around deadlines. Part-time describes the number of hours, not whether the schedule is flexible. A part-time job may have a fixed schedule, while a flexible arrangement may still be full-time.

A posting should identify the weekly hours or deadlines, required availability, time zone and who sets the schedule. The guide to reading a job posting covers the other details to compare.

Contract work may provide scheduling flexibility, but income and workload can vary. Deadlines, expected availability and the method of payment belong in the written agreement.

Asking your employer for flexible hours

A request for a different schedule may be governed by employment standards, workplace policy, a collective agreement or an employment contract. The applicable provincial, territorial or federal authority provides the current rules.
