How the work is delivered

Online work in Canada

Online means the work is completed or delivered over the internet. It does not always mean that a person can work from home or from any province.

At a glance

Typical tasks
Support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, moderation, software.
Status
Employee or independent contractor. The wording in a job posting does not determine your legal status.
Pay
Hourly, salary, or per project. An advertised figure is not yet your offer.
Additional work
May include part-time support, writing, bookkeeping, design, tutoring or software work.

Reviewed October 2026. This is not a job posting.

Online work is not necessarily remote work

Online work can be done at an employer’s workplace or from another location. Remote work can also include scheduled phone-based duties that people may not normally describe as online work. On North Desk, online work means tasks that are completed or delivered over the internet. Always check the work location stated in the job posting.

Employee or contractor

Some online work is employment, with a schedule, a manager and a pay stub. Canada Pension Plan and Employment Insurance deductions may apply. Other online work is contract work: you send invoices, take on financial risk and manage your own taxes. The Canada Revenue Agency considers factors such as control, tools, financial risk and opportunity for profit. Describing a position as freelance does not settle the question. Check with the CRA if your status is unclear. This page is not tax advice.

Work people add beside a main job

Online work added alongside a main job is often familiar work done over the internet for part of the week: customer service, bookkeeping, writing, design, tutoring, moderation or software development. Consider which skills you can already demonstrate and whether the hours fit your schedule. The resume guide explains how to present that experience.

Software development is one occupation where online delivery is common. In the second quarter of 2026, Statistics Canada counted 3,545 vacancies for software developers and programmers, up about 35 per cent year over year, with an average offered wage of $43.65 an hour. That figure describes wages offered for vacant positions in the occupation. It is not a standard remote-work wage or an offer to you.

A job posting should name the organization, describe the work and explain how you would be paid. When you are ready to search, use Job Bank’s remote filter and check the employer’s own careers page.
