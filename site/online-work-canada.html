How the work is delivered

Online work in Canada

Online work is completed or delivered over the internet. Work location is a separate condition and may be limited to a province.

At a glance

Typical tasks
Support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, moderation, software.
Status
Employee or independent contractor. The label in a posting does not determine legal status.
Pay
Hourly, salaried or project-based. An advertised figure is not a written offer.
Additional work
Often completed part-time alongside a main job or other commitments.

Last reviewed October 2026.

Online work is not necessarily remote work

Online work can be completed at an employer’s workplace or somewhere else. Remote work can also include scheduled phone duties that are not usually described as online work. On this site, online work means tasks completed or delivered over the internet. Work location remains a separate detail in the posting.

Employee or contractor

Some online roles are employment, with scheduled hours, supervision, pay statements and payroll deductions where applicable. Others are independent contract work involving invoices, financial risk and self-employed tax obligations. The Canada Revenue Agency considers factors such as control, tools, financial risk and opportunity for profit. The word “freelance” does not determine employment status.

Online work beside a main job

Online work alongside a main job often uses existing experience in customer service, bookkeeping, writing, design, tutoring, moderation or software development. Demonstrated skills and available hours help determine whether a posting is suitable. The résumé guide covers how that experience can be presented.

Software development is one occupation where online delivery is common. In the second quarter of 2026, Statistics Canada counted 3,545 vacancies for software developers and programmers, up about 35 per cent year over year, with an average offered wage of $43.65 an hour. That figure is the average advertised wage for vacancies in the occupation, not for remote work generally.

A clear posting names the organization, describes the work and states whether pay is hourly, salaried or project-based. Job Bank searches by occupation and work location; the employer’s careers page can confirm the same posting. The remote-work search guide covers the available filters.
