How the work is delivered

Online work in Canada

Online means the work is completed or delivered over the internet. It does not always mean that a person can work from home or from any province.

At a glance

Typical tasks
Support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, moderation, software.
Status
Employee or independent contractor. The label on the ad is not the legal test.
Pay
Hourly, salary, or per project. An advertised figure is not yet your offer.
Second path
Often a shorter week of support, writing, books, design, tutoring or software.

Reviewed October 2026. Not a vacancy.

Online is not the same word as remote

A person can do online work in an office. A person can also do remote work that is not “online” in the casual sense, such as a phone roster with set shifts. When North Desk says online, we mean the deliverable travels by internet and the seat is not an employer’s floor. If the ad also requires a city, read it as a location rule, not as a mood.

Employee or contractor

Some online work is employment: a schedule, a manager, a pay stub, Canada Pension Plan and Employment Insurance when the rules say so. Some is contract work: you invoice, you carry the risk of a quiet month, and you handle your own tax. The Canada Revenue Agency looks at control, tools, financial risk and the chance of profit. Calling it freelance in the posting does not decide the question. Confirm with CRA if the status is unclear. This page is not tax advice.

Work people add beside a main job

A second path online is usually an ordinary occupation done over the internet for part of the week: answering customers, keeping books, writing, designing, tutoring, moderating, or building software. The useful question is whether you can already do a piece of that work, and whether the hours fit. The CV guide is about showing that piece of work.

Software development is one occupation where online delivery is common. In the second quarter of 2026, Statistics Canada counted 3,545 vacancies for software developers and programmers, up about 35 per cent on the year, with an average offered wage of $43.65 an hour. That is the wage advertised on empty jobs in that occupation. It is not a remote salary, and it is not an offer to you.

Recruitment for this work still names an organisation, a task, and how you are paid. When you are ready to look, use Job Bank’s remote filter and the employer’s own careers page.
