Online work in Canada

Online means the work moves over the internet. It is a way of delivering the job, not a promise that you can do it from any kitchen table.

At a glance

Typical tasks
Support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, moderation, software.
Status
Employee or independent contractor. The label on the ad is not the legal test.
Pay
Hourly, salary, or per project. Never a fee you pay first.
Watch for
Simple “tasks” inside an app, with a balance you cannot withdraw.

Reviewed October 2026. Not a vacancy.

Online is not the same word as remote

A person can do online work in an office. A person can also do remote work that is not “online” in the casual sense, such as a phone roster with set shifts. When North Desk says online, we mean the deliverable travels by internet and the seat is not an employer’s floor. If the ad also requires a city, read it as a location rule, not as a mood.

Employee or contractor

Some online work is employment: a schedule, a manager, a pay stub, Canada Pension Plan and Employment Insurance when the rules say so. Some is contract work: you invoice, you carry the risk of a quiet month, and you handle your own tax. The Canada Revenue Agency looks at control, tools, financial risk and the chance of profit. Calling it freelance in the posting does not decide the question. Confirm with CRA if the status is unclear. This page is not tax advice.

Recruitment versus a task feed

Recruitment ends with an organisation you can phone and a written description of the work. A task feed starts with a message, an app, and a balance on a screen. The Canadian Anti-Fraud Centre describes the second pattern under crypto job fraud, product boosting, web surveys and fake online-store jobs. Read the scam briefing before you install anything.

Software development is one occupation where online delivery is common, and where vacancies rose. In the second quarter of 2026, Statistics Canada counted 3,545 vacancies for software developers and programmers, up about 35 per cent on the year, with an average offered wage of $43.65 an hour. That is the wage on empty jobs in that occupation. It is not a remote salary, and it is not an offer.
