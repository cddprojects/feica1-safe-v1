How the work is delivered

Online work in Canada

Online work is completed or delivered over the internet. Work location is a separate condition and may be limited to a province.

At a glance

Typical tasks
Support, writing, design, bookkeeping, tutoring, moderation, software.
Status
Employee or independent contractor. The label in a posting does not determine legal status.
Pay
Hourly, salaried or project-based. An advertised figure is not a written offer.
Additional work
Part-time support, writing, bookkeeping, design, tutoring or coding.

Last reviewed October 2026.

Online work is not necessarily remote work

Online work can be done from an employer’s office or somewhere else. Remote work can also include scheduled phone duties that are not usually described as online work. Here, online work means tasks completed or delivered over the internet. Work location is a separate detail in the posting.

Employee or contractor

Some online jobs are regular employment: you get a schedule, a manager and a pay stub, and Canada Pension Plan and Employment Insurance deductions may apply. Others are contract work, where you send invoices, carry the financial risk and handle your own taxes. The Canada Revenue Agency considers factors such as control, tools, financial risk and opportunity for profit. The word “freelance” does not determine employment status.

Online work beside a main job

Many people add online work to a full-time job by doing something they already know for a few hours a week: customer service, bookkeeping, writing, design, tutoring, moderating or coding. Think about the skills you can already show and whether the hours really fit your week. Our resume guide shows how to present that experience.

Software development is one occupation where online delivery is common. In the second quarter of 2026, Statistics Canada counted 3,545 vacancies for software developers and programmers, up about 35 per cent year over year, with an average offered wage of $43.65 an hour. That figure is the average advertised wage for vacancies in the occupation, not for remote work generally.

A good posting names the organization, describes the work and explains how you would be paid. Search Job Bank by occupation and work location, then confirm the posting on the employer’s careers page. See how to search for remote work.
