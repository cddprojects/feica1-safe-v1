Work from home, online work and side jobs: what the terms mean

North Desk · Last reviewed October 2026

People use these phrases for different kinds of work. Some describe a job arrangement. Others describe independent work or a general wish for more income. Knowing the difference makes the search more useful.

The short version

  • Work from home, part-time jobs and remote work usually belong to a job search.
  • Online work can mean employment or freelance work completed over the internet.
  • Freelance usually means providing a service to clients rather than joining an employer as an employee.
  • Side job means work held beside a main job, studies, care or another commitment.
  • Make money online, earn money from home and earn money online are broader searches. They are not job titles and do not describe a reliable level of income.

Terms that are closely related to jobs

Jobs is a broad word. A useful search pairs it with a real occupation, such as customer support, bookkeeping, writing or software, and then adds the work arrangement.

Work from home describes where the work is done. Indeed Canada notes that employers do not always use it in the same way. Some postings use it for fully remote work; others use it for occasional days at home. Read the location line on each posting.

Part-time describes hours. Statistics Canada uses fewer than 30 usual hours a week at the main or only job for its national labour survey. An employer still needs to state the actual schedule. Search for a job title and then use the hours filter.

Remote work means the job is not normally completed at the employer’s workplace. Job Bank separates remote, hybrid, on-site and on-the-road jobs. A remote posting may still require the worker to live in Canada or in a named province.

Terms that can include contract work

Online work describes how the work is completed or delivered. Support, writing, editing, bookkeeping, tutoring, design and software can all be online. The person may be an employee or an independent contractor, so the phrase alone does not tell you the pay, hours or legal status.

Freelance usually means work for clients, paid by the hour or by a project. Freelancers may need to find the next client, keep records and plan for tax. A freelance project is not automatically a part-time job with employee benefits.

Side job is ordinary language for work beside another commitment. Official Canadian sites are more likely to use part-time, casual, contract, self-employed or gig work. In the year to December 2025, 667,000 Canadians aged 15 to 69 did paid work through a digital platform. Only 21.8 per cent were doing that platform work as their main job or business in December. Platform work includes delivery and driving as well as online services, so it is not the same as remote work.

What the money phrases mean

Searches such as make money online, earn money from home and earn money online show an interest in income, but they do not identify an occupation or an employer. Results can include jobs, freelance services, selling products, surveys, creator work and investing. North Desk covers only the employment and work side of those searches.

No guide can promise that a person will make money online or earn a set amount from home. Pay depends on the work, the hours, the person’s experience, the province and whether the arrangement is employment or contract work. Start with a job title or a service you can provide, then add remote, part-time or freelance.

Better searches for work

  • Remote customer support jobs Canada — a job title, a work location and a country.
  • Part-time online bookkeeping — hours, delivery and a skill.
  • Freelance writing Canada — independent work and a service.
  • Work from home accounting Ontario — a work arrangement, occupation and province.

Job Bank recommends starting with a job title and location, then narrowing the results with filters for remote work, hours, salary and other needs. Indeed Canada also recommends using its Remote filter and checking whether a posting is tied to a province or city. North Desk explains the terms; it does not carry the openings.
