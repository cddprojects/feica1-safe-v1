Place

Remote work in Canada

Remote means the job is not tied to the employer’s office. It still has a province, a time zone and a payer.

At a glance

Place
Not the employer’s office. Hybrid is a named split, not “remote”.
Hours
Often full-time. Part-time in the national survey is usually under 30 hours.
Recruitment
Apply on Job Bank or the employer’s site. The offer names hours and place.
Where to look
Job Bank’s remote filter, and the employer’s own careers page.

Reviewed October 2026. Not a vacancy.

What the word is doing

On Job Bank and Indeed, a posting is marked on-site, hybrid or remote. Those are three agreements. Hybrid means some days in a stated place. Remote means you are not required to report to the office. A keyword search for “work from home” is not the same as the remote filter: it also returns on-site jobs that mention a home somewhere in the text.

Indeed’s own posting rules tell employers to use “Canada” or “nationwide” only when the person can do the work from anywhere in the country. Many so-called remote roles still say “must reside in Ontario” or “must be in a Canadian time zone.” That line is the real location.

How recruitment usually runs

A careful employer posts the role, takes applications, and has someone from the organisation speak with you. The offer names the pay, the hours, who employs you, and where you are allowed to work from. If this would be a second job, those hours are the part you compare with the week you already have.

North Desk does not carry those postings. When you want live ads, open Job Bank and filter for remote.

What to ask before you agree

  • Which provinces may I work from, and what happens if I move?
  • Is any day in an office required? If yes, it is hybrid.
  • Who employs me, and is the pay hourly, salary, or per project?
  • Which time zone are meetings in?
  • Who provides the computer and software?

Pay rules are covered in What a second paycheque changes. When you have a posting open, read how to check it with confidence.
