Location

Remote work in Canada

Remote work is performed away from the employer’s usual workplace. Some positions require residence in a specific province or availability in a stated Canadian time zone.

At a glance

Location
Away from the employer’s usual workplace. Hybrid jobs include some days on-site.
Hours
Often full-time. Statistics Canada counts part-time as under 30 hours a week.
Applying
Applications are submitted through Job Bank or the employer’s website after confirming hours and work location.
Where to look
Job Bank’s remote filter and the employer’s careers page.

Last reviewed October 2026.

What remote work means

On Job Bank and Indeed, a posting may be marked on-site, hybrid or remote. Hybrid combines work at a stated workplace with remote days. Remote means the worker is not normally required to report to the employer’s workplace. A keyword search for “work from home” can also return on-site positions that mention a home.

Indeed’s employer guidance reserves “Canada” or “nationwide” for work that can be performed anywhere in the country. Some remote jobs still require residence in a particular province or availability in a Canadian time zone. Location requirements should be confirmed before applying.

How employers hire for remote positions

Most employers post the job, review applications and arrange interviews, much like any other hire. A written offer normally sets out the pay, hours, employer and permitted work location. For a second job, stated hours must fit alongside existing employment or other commitments.

Current openings are listed through Job Bank’s remote-work filter and employers’ careers pages.

Details to confirm in an offer

  • Permitted provinces or territories and any relocation conditions
  • Required on-site days in a hybrid arrangement
  • Employer name and whether pay is hourly, salaried or per project
  • Time zone for meetings and required availability
  • Responsibility for computer equipment and software

The guides on pay for additional work and remote job postings cover province, employer, pay and hours in more detail.
