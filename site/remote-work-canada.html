Location

Remote work in Canada

Remote work is done away from the employer’s regular workplace. The position may still have provincial, territorial or time-zone requirements.

At a glance

Location
Away from the employer’s regular workplace. Hybrid positions include some on-site work.
Hours
Often full-time. Part-time in the national survey is usually under 30 hours.
Applying
Apply through Job Bank or the employer’s website. Confirm the hours and work location.
Where to look
Job Bank’s remote filter, and the employer’s own careers page.

Reviewed October 2026. This is not a job posting.

What remote work means

On Job Bank and Indeed, a job posting may be marked on-site, hybrid or remote. Hybrid means you work at a stated location some of the time. Remote means you are not normally required to report to the employer’s workplace. A keyword search for “work from home” is not the same as using the remote filter: it can also return on-site positions that happen to mention a home.

Indeed’s posting rules tell employers to use “Canada” or “nationwide” only when someone can do the work from anywhere in the country. Many remote positions still require applicants to live in a particular province or work in a Canadian time zone. Check the location requirements before applying.

How employers hire for remote positions

An employer usually posts the position, reviews applications and arranges interviews. A written offer should state the pay, hours, employer and permitted work location. If this would be a second job, compare the proposed hours with your current schedule.

Job Compass does not publish job postings. To see current opportunities, open Job Bank and use the remote-work filter.

What to ask before you agree

  • Which provinces or territories may I work from, and would moving affect the arrangement?
  • Am I required to work on-site on any days? If so, the position is hybrid.
  • Who employs me, and is the pay hourly, salary, or per project?
  • Which time zone applies to meetings and working hours?
  • Who provides the computer and software?

Pay rules are covered in What a second paycheque changes. When you have a posting open, read how to check it.
