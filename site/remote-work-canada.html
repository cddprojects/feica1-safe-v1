Location

Remote work in Canada

With remote work you do the job away from the employer’s office. Some remote jobs still need you to live in a certain province or work in a Canadian time zone.

At a glance

Location
Away from the employer’s office. Hybrid jobs include some days on-site.
Hours
Often full-time. Statistics Canada counts part-time as under 30 hours a week.
Applying
Apply through Job Bank or the employer’s website, and confirm the hours and where the work is done.
Where to look
Job Bank’s remote filter and the employer’s careers page.

Last reviewed October 2026. This page is a guide, not a job posting.

What remote work means

On Job Bank and Indeed, a job posting may be marked on-site, hybrid or remote. Hybrid means you work at a stated location some of the time. Remote means you are not normally required to report to the employer’s workplace. A keyword search for “work from home” is not the same as using the remote filter: it can also return on-site positions that happen to mention a home.

Indeed asks employers to use “Canada” or “nationwide” only when the work really can be done from anywhere in the country. Plenty of remote jobs still want you to live in one province or work in a Canadian time zone, so read the location details before you apply.

How employers hire for remote positions

Most employers post the job, review applications and book interviews, much like any other hire. A written offer should spell out the pay, hours, employer and where you are allowed to work. If this would be a second job, line the hours up with your current schedule first.

We don’t post jobs ourselves. To see what’s open right now, try Job Bank with the remote-work filter on.

Questions worth asking before you say yes

  • Which provinces or territories can I work from, and what happens if I move?
  • Do I have to come in on any days? If so, it’s a hybrid job.
  • Who is my employer, and is the pay hourly, salaried or per project?
  • Which time zone do the meetings and work hours follow?
  • Who supplies the computer and software?
