Location

Remote work in Canada

Remote work is performed away from the employer’s workplace. Some positions still require residence in a stated province or territory, or availability in a Canadian time zone.

At a glance

Location
Away from the employer’s workplace. Hybrid jobs include some days on-site.
Hours
May be full-time or part-time; the posting should state the schedule and time zone.
Applying
The application method should appear in a verified posting on Job Bank or the employer’s website.
Where to look
Job Bank’s remote filter and the employer’s careers page.

Last reviewed October 2026.

What remote work means

Job sites may label positions on-site, hybrid or remote. Hybrid positions require some attendance at a stated workplace. Remote positions are completed away from the employer’s workplace, but they may still be limited to a province, territory or time zone.

A search for “work from home” is less precise than a work-location filter because the phrase can also appear in hybrid or on-site postings.

How employers hire for remote positions

Remote hiring generally follows the same process as other hiring: application, review, interview and written offer. The offer should identify the employer, pay, hours and permitted work location. The posting should state required hours, time zone and availability.

Current openings are available through the work-location filter on Job Bank’s Find a job page.

Details to confirm in an offer

  • Permitted province or territory
  • Required on-site days
  • Employer identity and pay basis
  • Working time zone and required availability
  • Responsibility for equipment and software

The guides on pay and deductions and remote job postings cover the employer, location, pay and hours in more detail.
